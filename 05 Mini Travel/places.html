Famous Places in India

Taj Mahal, Agra

A white marble monument built by Emperor Shah Jahan in memory of his wife Mumtaz Mahal. It is one of the Seven Wonders of the World.

Hawa Mahal, Jaipur

Also called the Palace of Winds, this pink sandstone building has hundreds of small windows and is a symbol of Jaipur.

Kerala Backwaters

A calm network of lakes and canals where visitors can enjoy a peaceful houseboat ride surrounded by palm trees.

Golden Temple, Amritsar

The holiest Sikh shrine, covered in gold and surrounded by a sacred pool. Free community meals are served to everyone.

Varanasi Ghats

One of the oldest living cities in the world, famous for its riverside steps on the Ganga and the evening aarti.
