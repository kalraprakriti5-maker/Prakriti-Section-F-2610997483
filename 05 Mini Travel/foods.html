Famous Indian Foods

Indian cuisine is famous all over the world for its rich flavours, colourful spices and variety. Every state has its own special dishes, from creamy curries in the north to crispy dosas in the south. Rice, wheat, lentils and fresh vegetables are used in many meals, and spices like turmeric, cumin, cardamom and chilli make the food unique.

About Some Dishes

Biryani
A fragrant rice dish cooked with spices and meat or vegetables.
Masala Dosa
A thin, crispy rice pancake filled with spicy potato and served with chutney and sambar.
Pani Puri
Small crispy shells filled with spicy, tangy water, potatoes and chickpeas.
Gulab Jamun
Soft, fried milk balls soaked in sweet sugar syrup.
